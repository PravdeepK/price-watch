How it works
  • Type what you want in the box at the top to search Best Buy, Walmart, Canadian Tire, Newegg, Canada Computers, Amazon, Steam and Bethesda Gear at once. Pick a result to add it. You can also paste any product link.
  • Prices are checked every 30 minutes. You get an alert on your phone when a price drops, or the first time it reaches your target.
  • Minimum drop (in Settings on each card) skips small changes: 5 means $5 or more, 5% means 5 percent or more.
  • Games with several editions are one card. Each edition has its own price and alerts.
  • 🔔 on a picture mutes an item: still checked, no alerts. Pause (in Settings) stops checking it.
  • 🛒 On Shopify stores (for example Bethesda Gear), alerts and cards have Buy now (straight to guest checkout) and Add to cart buttons. You still finish checkout yourself.
  • 📦 Back in stock alert (when adding, or in Settings): an urgent alert when a sold out item can be bought again. Works on stores that show stock (Shopify shops, Best Buy, Canadian Tire, Walmart, Newegg, Amazon, and most stores with product data).
  • If a price cannot be read, you get one "Cannot read" alert. Open Settings on the card to fix it with a CSS selector.
  • Links must be Canadian stores in CAD. Other links can still be added with Test it anyway.
  • Press / to paste a link, 1–3 to switch tabs, ? for all shortcuts.
